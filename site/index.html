Anecdote.

you’re better at this than you think — you just need a head start.

is "not much" your default answer when someone asks what you've been up to? the stories are in you, waiting to be shared, but the words don't come in time.

anecdote turns anything on your mind into a few natural talking points. no scripts, no rehearsing — just something real to reach for, so you can get started and let the rest... flow.

how it works

  1. input a topic — type a brief summary of something you did or thought about recently (e.g. trying a new hobby or going on a trip).
  2. follow-up — one clarifying question to draw out the most interesting or vivid detail of your experience.
  3. talking points — your story broken into easy-to-remember parts:
    • hook — a compelling opener to grab the listener's attention.
    • detail — a concrete, vivid, or sensory element that makes the story memorable.
    • point / turn — the ultimate takeaway, verdict, or an unexpected twist.
    • toss-back — a natural question to hand the conversation back and keep it moving.
  4. more angles (optional) — tap "more angles" for a few other perspectives on the same topic, in case you want another way in.
  5. save the card — keep the story in your library, so it's there when you need it.
  6. not quite? try again — get a fresh take on the same story, as many times as you like.
  7. tell me what you think — there's a "something missing? tell me what to fix" link at the bottom of the chat. every note comes straight to me.
